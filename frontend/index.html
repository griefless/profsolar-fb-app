💡 Posting Strategy Tips

📌 Post 2x Daily on Weekdays

Morning post (7-9 AM) catches people checking phones before work. Evening post (6-9 PM) catches people relaxing at home during load shedding.

📌 Weekend Strategy

Post once on weekends, preferably late morning or early evening. Weekends are great for longer-form content and engagement posts.

📌 Load Shedding Bonus

Post IMMEDIATELY when load shedding is announced. These posts get 5x normal engagement as people search for solutions.

📌 Consistency is Key

Facebook's algorithm rewards consistent posting. Aim for 10-14 posts per week minimum. Use the auto-scheduler to maintain consistency.
